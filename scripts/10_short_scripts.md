# Form and Fire — First 10 Short Scripts

**Channel:** Form and Fire  
**Format:** Girl exercises silently + Voiceover (ElevenLabs) + Clean subtitles  
**Style:** Clean, premium, instructional  
**Length:** 25–35 seconds each

---

## Script 1 — Most Common Squat Mistake

**Title:** Most girls do squats wrong

**Visual:**
- Girl performs bodyweight squats
- Show incorrect form first (knees caving in / going too far forward)
- Then correct form (hips back, knees tracking over toes)

**Voiceover:**
Most girls do squats completely wrong.  
They let their knees shoot forward and lose tension in the glutes.  
Instead, push your hips back like you’re sitting on a chair.  
Keep your chest up and drive through your heels.  
Feel the difference.

## Script 2 — Why Your Glutes Aren’t Growing

**Title:** Why your glutes aren’t growing

**Visual:**
- Girl performing glute bridge
- Close-up on the peak contraction
- Slow motion of the squeeze at the top

**Voiceover:**
You’re doing hundreds of glute bridges…  
but your glutes still aren’t growing.  
Here’s why:  
You’re just moving the weight up and down.  
At the top — pause and squeeze hard for two full seconds.  
That’s where the growth happens.

## Script 4 — How to Do Lunges Correctly

**Title:** You’re doing lunges wrong

**Visual:**
- Girl performing reverse lunges
- Front and side view
- Show common mistake (front knee collapsing) vs correct form

**Voiceover:**
Most people turn lunges into a quad exercise.  
If you want glutes, take a longer step back.  
Keep your front knee stacked over your ankle.  
And push through the heel of the front foot.  
Feel it in the right place.

## Script 5 — Core Exercise Without Hurting Your Lower Back

**Title:** Train your core without hurting your back

**Visual:**
- Girl performing dead bug or hollow body hold
- Controlled movement, no momentum

**Voiceover:**
Crunches are destroying your lower back.  
Here’s a better way:  
Dead bugs.  
Keep your lower back pressed into the floor the entire time.  
Move slowly and with control.  
This builds real core strength without the pain.

## Script 9 — The Real Reason Your Progress Stopped

**Title:** Why you stopped seeing results

You’re doing the same workouts every week…  
and wondering why nothing is changing.

## Script 10 — Simple Rule for Better Form

**Title:** One rule that fixes almost every exercise

**Visual:**
- Girl demonstrating controlled tempo on different movements

**Voiceover:**
Here’s one simple rule that improves almost every exercise:  
Control the negative.  
Don’t just drop the weight.  
Take three full seconds on the way down.  
This creates more tension…  
and more results.
